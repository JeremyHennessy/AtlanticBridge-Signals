# Canada-wide market scope update — 22 September 2026

## Approved baseline

Starting production baseline: `c2cba1c9fee64bb8a961ff290dbc2a244bcf81b4` (PR #88). That baseline remains the reference for the accepted purpose-led UI, historical evidence payload, source boundaries, and browser behavior.

## Scope correction

AtlanticBridge Signals is not a Nova-Scotia-only expansion product.

The primary commercial question is whether a European company is establishing, acquiring, hiring, investing, partnering, or materially expanding in **Canada**. Geographic fit is a second and independent question: **which Canadian province, region, or metro is supported by location-specific evidence?**

Nova Scotia remains a valid market lens when evidence supports it, but it is one market among many and must not be treated as the default Canadian destination.

## UI change

- Added a Canada-wide **Markets** view.
- Reframed the overview from "Canada expansion + Nova Scotia landing" to "Canada expansion + Canadian market fit".
- Preserved Expansion Likelihood, market fit, and evidence confidence as separate concepts.
- Explicitly represented Atlantic Canada, Québec, Ontario, the Prairies, British Columbia, and Northern Canada as independent market lenses.
- Kept regional fit unscored until location-specific evidence and validation exist.
- Updated the user guide and evidence-boundary language to state that national Canadian evidence does not establish a province or city recommendation.

## Unchanged evidence and research

This scope update changes no collector, source evidence, accepted case payload, case classification, identity approval, publication timing, backtest, model label, or score. No regional ranking or market-fit recommendation is inferred from missing evidence.

The existing audited historical-case console remains a research preview rather than a validated live expansion-scoring product.
