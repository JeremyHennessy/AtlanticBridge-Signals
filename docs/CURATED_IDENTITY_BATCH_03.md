## Boundaries

These decisions confirm the named legal entity or parent relationship required by the specific queue task.

They do not independently establish:
- first Canadian presence;
- beneficial ownership beyond the cited relationship;
- an Expansion Likelihood score.

Evidence-document identifiers such as a CIPO application number are not treated as corporate-registry identifiers.
